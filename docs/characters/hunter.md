GD-SURVEY Hunter Robot

Canon written from `hunter.png`, the owner's reference (2026-09-12, bead
godot-test1-hb0: "scary robots, like this Robocop"). The picture is the record;
this file is what it says, so that the next person to touch
`scripts/generate_hunter.py` retunes toward the same machine instead of toward
their own idea of one. The SAME chassis is the field hunter and the HQ's tower
guard — one body, two duties.

Overall: a bulky armoured biped, a full head taller than a hero (~2.5 m), and
built like a person wearing several hundred kilos of plate rather than like a
machine pretending to be a person. Heavy, slow-looking, upright, symmetrical.
Nothing about it is sleek. The fear it carries is that it is shaped like
somebody and is far too big.

Head: a smooth steel DOME, small against the shoulders, on a short thick neck.
One horizontal AMBER visor slit across the face, lit, with a dark hood/brow over
it — one bar, never two eyes. Under the visor a dark chin grille (a corrugated
vent in the reference). No mouth, no features.

Shoulders: large rounded pauldrons, the widest part of the machine (~1.3 m
across), topping out level with or just above the chest so the head sits in a
notch between them.

Chest: a riveted barrel plate, lighter than the panels around it, carrying two
marks:
  * the GD-SURVEY emblem — a crossed FORK AND SPOON, blue and gold, worn where
    a soldier wears a unit patch;
  * under it, a pale ID PLATE with dark lettering ("GD-SURVEY" in the
    reference). It has to read as a stamped plate at five metres; the letters
    themselves do not have to be legible.
Under the chest the waist is dark and narrow — the gap between the torso and
the hips is one of the strongest reads in the picture.

Arms: heavy blocks that get BIGGER toward the hand. Block upper arm, a dark
elbow joint, an oversized forearm, and a fingerless SLAB FIST hanging to
mid-thigh. No hands, no fingers, no weapon.

Legs: thick armoured thighs, a knee plate bolted across the joint, thick
calves, and heavy boots with a wide sole. Stance slightly wide. Nothing tapers:
a leg that narrows toward the floor reads as an animal's.

Livery: brushed steel / grey-beige panels, everything articulated in dark
near-black seams, joints and edge lines, with rivet studs along the plate
edges. The paint is the corporate tell — nothing else in the field is painted.

Not in the picture and not in the model: no tail, no visible weapon, no wheels
or treads, no tubes or wiring, no exposed machinery. It is armour over nothing.

Generator notes (what the model rests on, so a retune does not lose it):
  * The three greys are three VALUES, not three tints — plate, mid panel and
    near-black joint, alternating down every limb. Two greys a stop apart weld
    into one silhouette under the game's white sky.
  * The waist stays black: it is what stops the forearms merging into the hips.
  * The chest stays NARROWER than the shoulders, or the torso and both
    pauldrons read as one slab whose top edge looks like the top of a head.
  * The amber slit only reads as a slit while it has a dark brow above it.
